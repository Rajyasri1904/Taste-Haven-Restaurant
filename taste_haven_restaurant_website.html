Our Culinary Story

Where Heritage Recipes Meet Modern Hearth Fire

Founded in 2016 by Chef Julian Mercer, Taste Haven began with a simple conviction: extraordinary meals start in healthy soil and finish over seasoned white-oak embers.

We partner with over 18 family-owned orchards, dairies, and organic farms within 50 miles of our kitchen—ensuring every pasta is rolled each morning and every sauce simmers from scratch without shortcuts.
